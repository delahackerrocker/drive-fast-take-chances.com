# Steven de la Torre Three-Page Master Resume Design

## Objective

Create a second, expanded master resume for Steven de la Torre in DOCX and PDF formats. This edition will extend the approved two-page resume to approximately three full US Letter pages while preserving its recruiter-friendly reading order, visual identity, factual accuracy, and applicant-tracking-system compatibility.

## Deliverables

- `output/resume/Steven_de_la_Torre_Master_Resume_3_Page.docx`
- `output/resume/Steven_de_la_Torre_Master_Resume_3_Page.pdf`

The existing two-page DOCX, PDF, and Markdown files will remain unchanged.

## Content Strategy

The three-page edition will use the supplied master resume brief as its factual source of truth. It will expand the two-page copy rather than inventing new claims.

Additional space will be used to:

- Restore fuller role detail for Imprezario Entertainment and Activision Blizzard / Raven Software
- Expand the Unity UX, virtual-reality, augmented-reality, and mixed-reality freelance work
- Preserve more client, product, franchise, and project context in older roles
- Give PRACT1T10N3R / GR1M01RE additional systems, tooling, accessibility, and AI-assisted-development detail
- Present the skills taxonomy with clearer category separation and less compression
- Preserve awards, education, certifications, and the AbleGamers accessibility credential

The resume will remain selective. It will not become an undifferentiated project list or include unsupported metrics, dates, technologies, team sizes, titles, or claims.

### Page 1

1. Name, professional title, contact information, portfolio, and LinkedIn

### Page 2

### Page 3

Manual page breaks will create predictable page ownership. Role headings, metadata, technology lines, and first bullets will remain together.

## Visual System

The second edition will reuse the approved Practitioner.Digital resume design:

- White printable page background
- Near-black body text
- Practitioner.Digital cyan `#12B5CD` behind major section titles
- Bold sans-serif name and role hierarchy
- Aptos/Arial-compatible typography
- Single-column reading flow
- Real text bullets and hyperlinks
- No floating text boxes, decorative icons, or essential header/footer content

The additional page will improve readability through slightly larger type and more generous paragraph spacing than the two-page edition while keeping the document concise and professional.

## ATS and Accessibility Requirements

- All essential content remains selectable text in normal reading order
- Bullets use real numbering definitions in DOCX and selectable glyphs in PDF
- Email, portfolio, and LinkedIn links remain clickable
- No essential content appears only in a shape, drawing, header, or footer
- Text extraction from both final formats must preserve all sections and role content
- The PDF must contain embedded or safely substituted fonts
- Both formats must contain exactly three nonblank US Letter pages

## Validation

The DOCX will be opened and visually inspected in Microsoft Word. The PDF will be rendered to PNG and inspected page by page. Structural checks will verify page geometry, real bullets, hyperlinks, text boxes, reading order, page count, font safety, selectable contact information, section completeness, and content parity.

The original two-page resume files and all unrelated repository files will remain unchanged.
