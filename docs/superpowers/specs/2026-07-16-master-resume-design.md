# Steven de la Torre Master Resume Design

## Objective

Create a polished, approximately two-page master resume for Steven de la Torre in DOCX, PDF, and editable Markdown formats. The resume should reflect the visual identity of Practitioner.Digital while remaining highly readable, printable, and compatible with applicant tracking systems.

## Deliverables

- `Steven_de_la_Torre_Master_Resume.docx`
- `Steven_de_la_Torre_Master_Resume.pdf`
- `Steven_de_la_Torre_Master_Resume.md`

All three files will be placed in a dedicated project output directory. The DOCX and PDF will contain matching content and closely matching layouts.

## Content

The supplied master resume brief is the factual source of truth. Editorial refinement may improve clarity, scanning, consistency, and page fit, but it must not introduce unsupported claims, technologies, dates, roles, metrics, or credentials.

The portfolio URL will be `https://practitioner.digital`. The retired `drive-fast-take-chances.com` address will not appear.

The resume will prioritize recent and relevant experience, especially Imprezario Entertainment, Activision Blizzard / Raven Software, freelance Unity and mixed-reality work, and PRACT1T10N3R / GR1M01RE. Older roles will be more concise so the complete document remains approximately two pages.

## Visual System

The resume will use an ATS-friendly, single-column structure with a restrained interpretation of the current website design:

- White page background and near-black text for print reliability
- Practitioner.Digital cyan (`#12B5CD`) as the principal accent
- Cyan background bars behind major section titles
- Bold sans-serif name and heading treatment inspired by the website
- Aptos or Arial-compatible body typography for dependable Word and PDF rendering
- Compact rules, spacing, and hierarchy without decorative icons or floating text boxes
- Strong contrast that remains readable in grayscale

The visual character should feel confident, direct, technical, and intentionally designed without becoming gimmicky or resembling a generic resume template.

## Layout

The document will use US Letter portrait pages with approximately 0.65-inch margins and a single primary reading column.

Page 1 will contain:

1. Name, professional title, contact details, portfolio, and LinkedIn
2. Professional summary
3. Core capabilities
4. Recent professional experience, beginning with Imprezario Entertainment and Activision Blizzard / Raven Software

Page 2 will contain:

1. Remaining professional experience
2. Selected independent work
3. Skills
4. Awards and recognition
5. Education and certifications

Role headings and their first bullets will remain together. The layout will avoid blank pages, orphaned lines, clipped bullets, and sections split in visually confusing places.

## ATS and Accessibility Requirements

- All important content will remain in the normal document reading flow
- Name and contact information will be selectable text
- Bullets will use real list formatting
- Links will be clickable and include meaningful visible text
- No essential information will exist only in headers, footers, shapes, images, or text boxes
- Text extraction from DOCX and PDF will be checked for completeness and reading order
- The PDF will use embedded or safely substituted fonts

## Validation

The DOCX will be rendered to page images and visually inspected. The PDF will also be rendered and inspected page by page. Validation will cover page count, margins, typography, alignment, date consistency, bullets, links, clipping, blank pages, grayscale readability, and content parity between formats.

The existing uncommitted changes in `src/styles/replica.css` will be preserved exactly and will not be modified by this task.
